The problem

Everyone else gets paid whether it works or not.

The way agencies charge is the reason small businesses stop hiring them. You pay for hours, decks, and discovery phases — and the thing either lands or it doesn't, but the invoice arrives either way. We took the invoice off the front end. If what we build doesn't move your number, that's our loss to eat, not yours.

  • $5–15k deposit before anything exists
  • Billed by the hour, so slow work pays better
  • Six weeks of discovery you paid to sit through

  • Results are "outside the scope of engagement"

Extra Mile

You buy the outcome

  • Nothing changes hands until it's live and working
  • One number, agreed in writing, that we're measured on

05

The fit

We say no to most of the businesses we speak to.

Building for free only works if the thing genuinely pays off. So we're picky up front, and we'd rather tell you on the first call than six weeks in.

Good fit

  • You're doing real revenue and the bottleneck is time, not demand
  • Something repetitive eats hours every single week
  • You can name a number that would change your year
  • Someone on your side can answer questions for an hour a week
  • You'd keep a tool that worked, and pay for it happily

Bad fit

  • You want a logo, a brand book, or a rebrand
  • The problem is that nobody knows you exist yet
  • Success can't be measured in anything but a feeling
  • You need it live next Tuesday
  • You want the code handed over and the relationship ended
06

Questions

The things owners ask on the first call.

What's the catch?

We're betting that we can build something that works, and we're pricing that bet ourselves. That means we turn down jobs where we can't see the payoff clearly, and we're strict about agreeing on the number before we start. If we take your job on, it's because we expect to get paid.

Who decides whether it worked?

The number does. We write down your baseline before we start and measure the same thing 30 days after go-live, out of your own systems — your call logs, your calendar, your books. No interpretation, no argument.

What if it doesn't hit the number?

You owe nothing. You tell us to switch it off, or you let us keep working on it at our expense until it lands. Your call, either way.

How long does a build take?

Two to three weeks for the first one, from the day we agree on scope. Bigger builds get split so something useful is live inside three weeks, rather than everything arriving in month four.

Do I have to change the systems I already use?

No. We build around your phone, your calendar, your inbox and your point of sale. If your team has to learn a new system to get the benefit, we've built the wrong thing.

What happens if I stop paying?

The monthly is cancellable any time with 30 days' notice. We shut things down gracefully and export your data — no hostage-taking, no exit fee.

Who actually does the work?

The person you meet on the first call is the person who builds it. There's no account manager between you and the work, and nothing gets passed to a junior team you never met.
